Prototype 001

Basalt Holds Heat

Interactive thermal sculpture, synthetic data
Data disappears quickly. Its heat lingers.

Basalt is dense volcanic rock that can absorb heat and release it slowly. Here it becomes a model for computation: an invisible request warms a system, and the consequence remains after the work is done.

Curator's note

A physical memory for computation

A request may be invisible, but energy was spent, hardware warmed, and cooling systems responded. This prototype makes that hidden process visible without pretending to measure a real data center.

The rock remembers the fire by giving it back slowly.
